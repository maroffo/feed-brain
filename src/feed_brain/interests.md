# ABOUTME: Tiers drive bulletin selection (high/medium in, low out); sections are separate.

## Interest profile

**High interest (tier: high)**
- AI agents, LLM tooling, context engineering, MCP, memory systems; Claude Code, skills, subagents, hooks, AI-assisted development
- Security with real impact: actively exploited vulnerabilities, major breaches and ransomware campaigns (especially hitting Italy or the EU), analysis from primary sources (Krebs, Schneier, CSIRT Italia, ENISA, CISA advisories)
- Software architecture and distributed systems: patterns, scalability case studies, Go concurrency and performance
- Engineering management: productivity frameworks (SPACE, DORA), technical debt strategies, org design
- DevOps and cloud architecture: Kubernetes, Terraform, observability, significant GCP/AWS platform changes
- Italian and European politics, geopolitics, trade policy; justice system and prison reform
- Economics with data or original analysis: Italian public finance, labor, EU economy

**Medium interest (tier: medium)**
- Tech industry news with substance: regulation, antitrust, major outages, platform shifts
- Python tooling, CLI tools, terminal workflows; Docker, containers, security hardening
- Engineering leadership, hiring, team practices
- Research papers in software engineering and AI with practical, reproducible results
- Long-form culture and essays, personal blogs with original thought, science skepticism
- Leisure the reader chose: long-form sport analysis (rugby, cycling, basketball, football tactics), jazz album reviews, comics

**Low interest (tier: low)**
- Single CVE entries and single ransomware victim claims, unless they hit widely used software or Italian organizations (then medium). Either way their section is threat_radar
- Vendor product announcements, changelogs, Workspace feature updates, minor releases
- Gadget reviews, deals, consumer electronics news
- Match reports and results without analysis
- Basic tutorials, beginner guides, marketing, business strategy (unless novel frameworks), hype pieces, AI doomerism, sales pitches
- Agile methodology, Scrum, project management

## Quality signals (raise the tier)
- Original analysis with data or experience
- Actionable frameworks or mental models
- Practical papers with reproducible results
- Contrarian views backed by evidence

## Anti-patterns (lower the tier)
- Clickbait, listicles without depth
- Rehashed news without analysis
- Product announcements disguised as articles
- Tutorial-style content without novel insight
